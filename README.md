# NeuroWrite-Smart-Pen-for-Objective-Handwriting-Assessment
Evaluating the sensor-based pen for early screening of dysgraphia and other neuromotor disorders 

NeuroWrite is a smart pen that uses advanced, intelligent features to help identify handwriting-related neurological and developmental disorders at a very early stage. This will allow the users of NeuroWrite to have an objective means of assessing a person’s handwriting behaviour through the analysis of sensor data, instead of relying solely on traditional, observational methods of evaluating handwriting.

The NeuroWrite pen contains two Force Sensitive Resistors (FSRs) to measure grip pressure and an MPU6050 to measure acceleration and gyroscopic movement while the user writes. Since the invention of NeuroWrite, handwriting data has been collected from persons with diagnosed dysgraphia and Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), as well as from persons without any known handwriting problems.

The data will then lead us to various parameter types (e.g., pressure variability; motion variability; movement smoothness, or jerk; tremor patterns; and pressure-motion coordination) that we can utilize as digital biomarkers of the user's neuromotor control during writing.

As a next step of the project, we will develop a system that can analyze the newly uploaded handwriting data and compare it to existing datasets to identify the patterns associated with the dysgraphia, ADHD, typical handwriting development, and or other possible neuromotor irregularities of the user. The system will use probability-based assessments to help the user assess and determine whether to seek additional evaluations from a medical professional.
